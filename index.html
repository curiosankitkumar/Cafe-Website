Our Story

Bread first, coffee always.

Willow & Whisk started in a 400-square-foot kitchen with a single stone deck oven. Today, our bakers still hand-shape each loaf, fold croissant dough overnight, and roast fruit for our jams in small batches.

We source heritage grains from family-run mills, partner with independent roasters, and design our menu around the seasons. Everything is made to slow mornings down.
